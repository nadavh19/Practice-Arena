# Piano sample attribution

The files `48.mp3` through `96.mp3` are the C3–C7 Acoustic Grand Piano samples from the FluidR3 General MIDI soundfont, converted and distributed by the [MIDI.js Soundfonts project](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts).

- FluidR3 samples: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (`LICENSE-FLUIDR3-CC-BY-3.0.txt`)
- MIDI.js Soundfonts project: Copyright © 2012 Benjamin Gleitzman, MIT License (`LICENSE-MIDIJS.txt`)
- Source path: `FluidR3_GM/acoustic_grand_piano-mp3`

The sample filenames were changed to MIDI note numbers for application lookup. The audio content is otherwise unmodified.
